# The interview

Ask in this order. Most people answer 1 to 10 in about 30 minutes; the rest are quick. Take answers from call notes when you have them, and ask only for what is missing. Write down their words: the plan uses them.

Each answer turns steps in `steps.md` on or off, or fills in a value the step needs.

| # | Ask | What the answer decides |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | In a normal week, what do you lose track of: new prospects and conversations, or orders, stock and payments? | **Fit.** Prospects: carry on. Orders, stock or payments: say ERPNext should come first, and CRM can follow. Both: ask which hurts more. |
| 1 | What does "CRM is working" look like for you a month from now? | Their live condition. It must be something you can check on data, not a date. |
| 2 | By when do you need it working? | The pace of the plan. Not the live condition. |
| 3 | What hurts most about how you sell today? | Which step to show working first. Lost follow-ups: `follow-ups`, `assignment`. No view of what will close: `forecasting`. |
| 4 | Walk me through one deal, from the first contact to payment. | `deal-stages`. A separate step to qualify an enquiry before it is a real deal: `lead-stages`. |
| 5 | How long does a deal usually take? How many new prospects a month? | When to expect `first-close`, and the numbers in `regular-use`. |
| 6 | How many people sell? Who manages them? Who handles which prospects? | `team`, `manager`. A named split (by source, area or product): `assignment`. |
| 7 | Where do prospects come from? | `sources`. Web forms, Facebook, marketplaces: note as an ask; they enter those by hand for now. |
| 8 | Why do you lose deals? | `lost-reasons`. |
| 9 | Besides customers, who else matters to a deal: partners, agents, banks, distributors? | `network`. |
| 10 | How do you talk to prospects: email, phone, WhatsApp? | Email: `email`, `templates`, `first-email`. Phone: `calls`. WhatsApp: note as an ask. |
| 11 | Which mailbox do you sell from, and who can change its settings? | How `email` is done. Nobody with admin access yet: move `email` to a later session. |
| 12 | Do you sell set offerings: products, plans or packages? | `products`. |
| 13 | One currency or several? | `company` currency. Several: `currencies`. |
| 14 | Which language does your team work in? | `team` language per user. |
| 15 | Where are your open prospects and deals today, and about how many? | `import`, and how: by hand or with Data Import. |
| 16 | Who looks at the pipeline, and how often? | `forecasting`, `pipeline-review`. |

## Asking well

- Ask one or two questions at a time, in plain words. Do not show this table.
- When an answer names a number, a name or a list, read it back.
- When they do not know, write "not known yet" and move on. Do not guess for them.
- A stage list over eight is usually two lists in one. Ask which stages a manager actually looks at.
